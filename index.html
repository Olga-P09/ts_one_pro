Why ThinkSmart One Pro

A better meeting room, without a complicated project.

What your customers get — and why it's an easy recommendation for you.

Heard distinctly

Eight beamforming mics with 180° coverage and AI noise suppression and echo cancellation — top-scoring in independent PAL Labs audio testing.

For customers: natural conversation, less crosstalk.

Seen clearly

Full HD camera with a 100° field of view, AI auto-framing and intelligent zoom, video fencing and a physical privacy shutter.

For customers: more equal hybrid meetings.

Fewer truck rolls

Intel® vPro® out-of-band management lets IT diagnose and recover rooms remotely — even when Windows or Teams isn't responding.

For IT: lower support and lifecycle costs.

Simple to deploy & scale

One table- or wall-mount bar, Windows Autopilot provisioning, and ThinkSmart Manager for real-time status, bulk updates and reporting from one console.

For partners: faster installs, repeatable rollouts.
